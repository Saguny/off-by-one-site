SAGUNY

GAME DEV · BACKEND

I have been very interested in games all my life. Overanalyzing everything I'd see in a game and wondering how something was made.

In late 2019 I started learning programming via a teacher that had been referred to me. She taught me many things related to web development and got me into my first internship at age 15 at Omexon, where I would stay for a year.

That was also when I had my first Discord bot project called Hugo.py, a simple Python-made Discord bot that was very heavily anime-themed and, not proud to admit, NSFW-related. It taught me many things: how to use APIs, what rate limiting is, what proper user experience means, request parsing, error handling, asynchronous programming and database management. To this day it has been heavily rewritten and stripped of its features and is now no longer in use.

Hugo also fueled my spark years later to create the famous Social Credit Surveillant with the help of Warp, a small funny idea that grew into a 100,000-user base. It taught me a lot about privacy and legals, lol.

That was also when I got inspired to make OFF-BY-ONE (name idea from Warp) and ultimately create this group and website. I adore Marathon's art style, so I spent about a whole night creating the site you see today.

By late 2023 I started university at the HSRW. That was also when I first got pulled into game development via the module "Einführung in die Medieninformatik." I had a blast working with others to create a short horror experience. I would spend day and night working on the project, fueling my perfectionism to get everything just how I want, terrorising my friends to playtest for me and give me suggestions. That would be my first project, yet unaware of what would come next.

Over the coming two and a half years I had a couple more game dev projects at university, which led up to today's game, Perennial, which we are currently developing. Always have been a crazy fan of space, so I am thrilled to make this a reality.

Stay wishy,
